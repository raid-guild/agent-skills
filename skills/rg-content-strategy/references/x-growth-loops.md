# X Growth Loops

Use this for X strategy, campaign planning, reply planning, and social draft audits.

These are heuristics, not guarantees. Do not let them override source discipline, account/persona fit, public safety, or operator approval.

## Cadence

Post more than feels comfortable when the account has enough sourced material.

- Prefer a steady flow of small, useful posts over waiting for one perfect post.
- Let strong posts reveal themselves through response and reuse.
- Keep a backlog of approved angles so the account is not blocked by overthinking.
- Do not fill the feed with weak or unsupported filler just to increase volume.

## Story Over Product

When a draft reads like feature shipping, look for the human story underneath:

- what changed for builders, members, clients, or users
- what ambition, coordination, relief, speed, taste, or confidence the work enables
- what tension existed before the solution
- what path the product, workflow, or service opens

For RaidGuild, translate product facts into builder-native stakes:

- what this lets a raid, cohort, client, member, or agent do next
- what problem got less annoying, less slow, or less opaque
- what proof shows the thing is real

Do not manufacture emotion. Ground story in actual source material.

## Timing

There is no perfect posting time.

- Use scheduling for operational consistency, campaign coordination, and reviewer convenience.
- Do not delay a strong, approved, time-sensitive post while hunting for an ideal slot.
- Treat timing as a minor optimization after source quality, angle, clarity, and engagement plan.

## Engagement

Plan for the conversation after the post.

- Reply to real responses when the account can add useful signal, appreciation, clarification, or a next question.
- Prefer questions that naturally extend the thread over generic engagement bait.
- Track good replies as future post seeds.
- Avoid arguing, overexplaining, or dragging private/internal context into public replies.

For reply candidates, include at least one option that asks a grounded follow-up question when appropriate.
